# Day 1: Take Action
Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Xq6-oO2n6-U (55 min). Read in full from the transcript.

A "contract" video. TJR says outright that no trading is taught here. Mindset only.

## Mindset rules
- **Commit to the whole boot camp**, every video, in order, plus the homework; re-watch lessons until they're understood.
- **No get-rich-quick.** Not a day, week or month: realistically 1–2 years to real money ("that is quick").
- **Forget everything you think you know** and start from the beginning; don't jump ahead ("what about a fair value gap? we'll get there").
- **Be real with yourself.** When a trade fails, it was *your* misread of the market, not the market and not the
  strategy. Don't switch strategies because of losses.
- **Market experience beats watching.** His biggest growth came from removing every indicator and simply watching
  price ("put time between yourself and the market"). Watching highlights doesn't teach you to play.
- **Journal everything.** "Let's make it a rule": keep a journal through the boot camp.
- Avoid signal groups, paid courses and "100% win rate" strategy videos. He calls them lies.

## The one trading insight in the video
- His first profitable edge, before he knew the name: the **"London fake-out"**, later recognised as a
  **liquidity sweep**. Trading GBP/JPY, at the London session open price would take out a high (sweep it) and then
  go the opposite way. He sold right after the high was taken, with a fixed 15-pip stop.
- He says he learned liquidity sweeps from market experience, not from ICT.

## For the bots
- Nothing to codify beyond: the sweep-and-reverse idea (detailed in Liquidity Pt 1–3) and keeping a journal (the
  `journal` bot already records every decision and its reason).
