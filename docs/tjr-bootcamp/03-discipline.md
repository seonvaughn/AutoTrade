# Day 3: Discipline
Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w2CWF0WtLmQ (34 min). Read in full from the transcript.

Discipline and habits; no chart work. The course alternates trading days and discipline days.

## Points relevant to trading
- "Me and you trade off the same strategy... the only thing that is different is our mindset." Students lose by bailing
  out of trades, lacking confidence to take valid trades, and so on. "The trading part is relatively easy."
- Professional traders trade **with risk management, no emotion and discipline**. "You can't treat this like a casino;
  you can't be over-trading, over-leveraging, because you'll never be profitable."
- When motivation fades (around day 8), discipline is all that's left.

## Habit-building method (his homework)
- Pick one positive habit that is *uncomfortable* for you (gym, reading 15–20 pages a day, a daily walk) and do it every day.
- About 21 days to form a habit; about 60 days for it to become routine.
- Picture the person you want to become and copy what they do every day. Aim to be 1% better every day.
- Optional: document the journey daily (photos or a video diary) for accountability.

## For the bots
- Nothing new to codify. "No over-trading, no over-leveraging" is already enforced: max trades per day, cooldowns, max
  open positions, premium-exposure cap, daily loss limit and kill switch.
