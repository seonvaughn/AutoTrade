# Day 5: Goal Setting
Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=N7so8LEHmLA (24 min). Read in full from the transcript.

Psychology day; no chart rules. The method (learned from a basketball program, "the X factor": the demo trader who
can't execute live):
1. Write **3 long-term end goals** (about 10 years out; at least one about trading).
2. For each, write **10 short-term goals or daily steps** that lead there. His trading examples: "I follow my
   trading plan every day", "I use risk management every day", "I journal my trades and review what I did right and
   wrong every day", "I'm profitable this week / this month".
3. Complete them daily, and **read and vocalize them at least 5 times a day in the present tense** ("I am a profitable
   trader", "I am patient within the markets"), rewriting them each morning in the notebook.
- Keep the notebook as your trading journal.

## For the bots
- Nothing to codify. The daily-step goals map onto what the bots already enforce every day: following the plan
  (config), risk management (risk limits) and journaling (the journal bot).
