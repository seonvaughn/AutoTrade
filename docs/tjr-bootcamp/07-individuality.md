# Day 7: Individuality
Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_6voYEdi5j4 (19 min). Read in full from the transcript.

Psychology day.
- Don't force a strategy that doesn't click. Take the pieces you understand (liquidity sweeps, BOS, FVGs, order blocks)
  and combine them; "my strategies are all just based off of simple concepts plugged into an execution order".
- His forex edge came from his own repetition: noticing the **London-open fake-out** (a strong move at the London
  open that immediately reverses) every session → his introduction to liquidity sweeps.
- It's a race with yourself; don't compare learning curves with friends.
- Confidence in life ↔ confidence to place your own trade against millions taking the other side.
