# Day 8: Liquidity Pt. 1
Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Lxnu7InGhGE (12 min). Read in full from the transcript.

"Probably the largest topic and the most important topic of my strategy." Part 1 = what and why; Part 2 = how to spot
it; Part 3 = how to trade it.

## Concepts
- **Liquidity = resting orders**: stop orders (taking people *out* of trades) and limit orders (buy and sell limits
  putting people *in*) sitting at certain price points. When price reaches such a point, lots of orders trigger:
  "a pool of money".
- **Why it moves the market:** banks and institutions ("market movers") have huge orders that need someone on the other
  side. They fill them by driving price into liquidity, where many traders are exiting or entering the opposite way.
  So price seeks liquidity, and the reversal *after* liquidity is taken is where the big move starts.
- He contrasts this with support and resistance ("it bounced off a floor so I buy" is guessing; he wants the *why*).
- **The trade logic (preview):** know where liquidity rests → price runs to it and takes it (the sweep) → wait for
  **confirmation: a break of structure / market structure shift** in the other direction (proof the orders were filled
  and direction changed) → enter. "If we don't see a shift in direction... hands off, we're not doing anything."
  Never buy or sell just because price reached liquidity.
- Done right, entries come at the **tops and bottoms of moves**, not mid-trend.
