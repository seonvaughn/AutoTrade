# Day 27: Fear and Doubt
Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TwakmPZJGJA (32 min). Read in full from the transcript.

## Concepts
- If you're new, trade on demo, where fear and doubt make no sense: a loss is free learning.
- **His path from demo to live:**
  1. Demo: at least **3 months profitable, at least 10% gain, one trade a day, 1% risk**.
  2. A tiny live account (e.g. $100, about $1 risk per trade), to get used to real wins and losses without emotion.
     About 90% of people blow the first live account, because the money gets to their head. If you do, go back to
     demo.
  3. Three more months profitable live.
  4. Then a funded (prop) account and one or two payouts, or your own capital only if you have over $10k you can
     truly afford to lose.
- "Profitable" means consistently taking money from the market, so demo wins don't count.
- Making 10% in a day still means you did something wrong (too much risk or too many trades).
- **The step-by-step strategy, proven over time, is what removes doubt.** Trading should be boring: one trade a day,
  1% risk, follow the plan, "be robotic".
