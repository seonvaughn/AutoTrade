# Day 31: Hard Work
Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=isetVuqlSLo (19 min). Read in full from the transcript.

A motivation lesson with no chart content. Points:
- Minimum effort gets minimum results. He points to an Alex Hormozi video: do far more work than the top people in
  your field, about four focused hours a day.
- His own path: hours of trading videos a day, 10+ trading books, 10+ psychology books.
- Embrace failure: fail many times and learn each time.
- Discipline outlasts motivation.
- Homework: rewatch Day 1 and revisit your goals.

  sessions before trading, score every signal forward, and keep testing.
