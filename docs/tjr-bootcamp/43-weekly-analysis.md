# Day 43: Weekly Analysis
Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9SUJfAo6Sl8 (10 min). Read in full from the transcript.

## Concepts
- **Start the week with the news calendar.**
  - Mark the no-trade days: CPI on Wednesday, PPI and unemployment claims on Thursday, both an hour before the open.
  - Watch the market but don't trade those days.
  - Consumer sentiment matters less.
  - Avoid the currencies involved around their releases.
- **Then top-down per instrument:** monthly, weekly, daily, 4H. Mark the building blocks (order blocks, FVGs,
  equilibrium, liquidity) and the likely draws for the week.
- **S&P example:**
  - Monthly BOS up ("not what I wanted, but it happened, so adapt").
  - Weekly and daily bullish. Price tested the daily order block and equilibrium, and the 4H broke up then came back.
  - Expect a sweep of nearby lows on Monday, then higher.
  - "**A 4H BOS means the daily is good to go.**"
  - Look for buys on Monday and Tuesday **as long as the 1H and 4H keep agreeing**.
- **Bias flips only on a break of structure** at the relevant timeframe. A monthly push higher is only confirmed by a
  weekly BOS. Until then, follow the weekly. "If you get proved wrong, you change your bias, you adapt."
- Weekly targets: the end of an imbalance, then the weekly liquidity pools.
